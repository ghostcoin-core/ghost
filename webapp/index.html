Claim

Move the remaining value, minus the fee, to a new key. The claim shows Sent as soon as the node takes it and Done at the first block that holds it; from that block on the received key shows claimed. If the chain is later reorganised and drops that block, Refresh says so and you can claim again. Confirmation trusts the chosen endpoint for the proof-of-work seal of each block, which the app does not verify.

Your new Phantom Key. Save it: after the claim it is the only authority over the coin.
