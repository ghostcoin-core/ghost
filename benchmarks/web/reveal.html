Coin reveal proving benchmark

One session per tab: open a fresh tab on this page, run one session, download the records, close the tab. The first proof of a session is the cold run; the rest are warm runs in the same worker. Proving and verification are timed in the worker with performance.now().

The prover is the real coin reveal statement of ghost-proofs in its shipped configuration (Keccak-256, blowup 2^2, zero-knowledge on), built from benchmarks/reveal. Download the proofs as well as the records to verify them natively with ghost-reveal-verify.
