THE LOGO
  A 100 x 100 box. Eight squares, each 26 x 26, at 0 / 37 / 74 (gaps of 11).
  The center cell holds the ghost: same width and height as a square, round top, three-point hem.
  Meaning: a ghost hidden among identical blocks. Your coins are one among many.

RULES
  Clear space    at least one square (26% of the logo's width) of empty space on every side
  Minimum size   16 px on screen, 6 mm in print; under 24 px use the one-color version
  Lockup         logo height = the height of the wordmark's "h"; gap = a third of the logo;
                 wordmark is always lowercase "ghost", Instrument Sans SemiBold, tracking -4.5%
  Never          outline it, color the squares differently, rotate it, stretch it, or add a face

COLORS

  Ink           #0E0D1A   text, logo squares
  Body text     #4A4860

  Ghost violet  #6B4CFF   the ghost, main buttons, links
  Violet tint   #EEEAFF   highlights (put ink text on it, not violet)
  Summon mint   #4FF0C8   only when coins come back; use as a fill with ink text, never as text on white
  Light-first: white pages, ink text, violet used sparingly. Dark backgrounds only for code boxes and merch.

TYPE
  Instrument Sans   everything people read (headlines SemiBold, tight tracking)
  IBM Plex Mono     keys, amounts, addresses, anything people copy or verify
